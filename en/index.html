What is Siddur Hayom?

Meet "Siddur HaYom" - your new daily companion for Jewish life, built with care to be with you from dawn until well after nightfall.

All of it wrapped in a modern, clean and fast design. Almost the whole app works with no internet (offline), so nothing stops your day.

"Siddur HaYom" is completely free and ad-free.

What you'll find in the app

📖

A smart, dynamic siddur

Ashkenaz, Sepharad, Edot HaMizrach or Chabad, in step with the Hebrew calendar and available offline

🌅

Real-time zmanim

A precise astronomical calculation for where you are, in Israel and abroad

🕍

Find a Minyan

Synagogues and minyanim near you, minyan reminders, and a call when your minyan is short

🍽️

Kosher Nearby

Kosher businesses in Israel and abroad, by distance

💧

Mikvaot Nearby

The nearest mikvaot, with opening hours and a map

🧭

A prayer compass

The exact direction of prayer from anywhere in Israel and the world

⏰

Alarms and reminders

An alarm clock made for Shabbat and holidays, and reminders for prayers, minyanim and Shabbat preparations

📚

A vast Jewish library

742 books, from Tanach to chassidut, all downloadable for offline use

🔖

Daily studies

Daf Yomi, Mishnah, Rambam, Tanya and more, with a "studied" checkmark and a progress calendar

📜

Psalms

The daily reading, Tikkun HaKlali and "My Psalm"

📅

Hebrew calendar

Holidays, weekly parashot and your own events, along with a date converter

🌾

Sefirat HaOmer

A daily reminder to count the Omer, with quick access to the blessing wording you chose

⏳

A Lock Screen timer

A countdown until Shabbat begins, a fast ends or the meat-to-dairy wait is over

📱

Widgets and shortcuts

Zmanim, Shabbat times, the current prayer and more, right from your home screen

👓

An advanced reading experience

Bookmarks, color themes, text search, full screen, automatic scrolling and more

🔍

Smart search

Finds any screen, prayer, book, time or setting, and sets an alarm or a reminder

👤

A personal account

Your bookmarks, studies and alerts on every device you sign in from

❤️

Completely Free

No ads, no payments, no subscriptions

Available for iPhone and Android, completely free
