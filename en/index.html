What is Siddur Hayom?

Meet "Siddur HaYom" - your new daily companion for Jewish life, built with care to be with you from dawn until well after nightfall.

All of it wrapped in a modern, clean and fast design. Almost the whole app works with no internet (offline), so nothing stops your day.

"Siddur HaYom" is completely free and ad-free.

What you'll find in the app

📖

A smart, dynamic siddur

The nusach of your prayers - Ashkenaz, Sepharad, Edot HaMizrach or Chabad - kept in step with the Hebrew calendar, with no internet either (offline).

🌅

Real-time zmanim

A precise astronomical calculation of every time of the day, done inside the app, from your GPS or from a city you pick, adapted for outside Israel and for those who live there.

🕍

Find a Minyan

Locate synagogues and minyanim near you, with no connection either, built on crowd wisdom and user verification. You can add reminders for specific minyanim, and send a call to people nearby when your minyan is short. Gabbaim can manage their synagogue and post notices and shiurim.

🍽️

Kosher Nearby

Kosher restaurants and businesses in Israel and abroad, by distance, with the kashrut type and certificate validity.

💧

Mikvaot Nearby

The nearest mikvaot, with opening hours and a map.

🧭

A prayer compass

It shows the exact direction of prayer from anywhere in Israel and the world. Calibration is available when needed.

⏰

An advanced alarm system

An alarm clock with a range of sounds, made for Shabbat and holidays, alongside reminders for Shabbat preparations, for times of the day you choose, for the meat-to-dairy wait and for prayers.

📚

A vast Jewish library

742 books, all of them downloadable for offline use, sorted into a range of categories: Tanach - including Tikkun Korim; Chazal - including the Talmud with the classic daf in a comfortable layout; Halacha - from the Rishonim to Pninei Halacha; responsa, Kabbalah, mussar, machshava and chassidut.

🔖

Daily studies

A wide range of daily studies within easy reach, with a "studied" checkmark and a personal progress calendar: Tehillim, Tikkun HaKlali, Daf Yomi in Bavli and Yerushalmi, Shnayim Mikra, Mishnah, Rambam, Shulchan Aruch, Aruch HaShulchan, Tanya, Tanach (traditional, 929 and Shnayim LeYom), the Haftarah, Daf HaShavua and Chok LeYisrael.

📜

Psalms

A screen with the daily reading by month or week, Tikkun HaKlali, and the "My Psalm" tile by age.

📅

Hebrew calendar

Holidays, weekly parashot and your own events, along with a date converter.

🌾

Sefirat HaOmer

A daily reminder to count the Omer, with quick access to the blessing wording you chose.

⏳

A Lock Screen timer

As you choose, a countdown until Shabbat or a holiday begins, until a fast ends, and until the meat-to-dairy wait is over - with no need to open the app.

📱

Widgets and shortcuts

Widgets - Siddur, Current Prayer, Shabbat times, zmanim, the calendar, the daily study, the nearest minyan and the meat-to-dairy wait. Shortcuts from the app icon - the alarm system, Find a Minyan, Birkat HaMazon, zmanim.

👓

An advanced reading experience

Bookmarks, Open Books, font and size, color themes, text search, margin width, full screen, automatic scrolling and more.

🔍

Smart search

Finds any screen, prayer, book, time or setting, and sets an alarm or a reminder.

👤

A personal account

Signing in gives you your bookmarks, your chosen studies, the preparation lists and the alerts on every device you sign in from.

❤️

Completely Free

No ads, no payments, no subscriptions

Available for iPhone and Android, completely free
